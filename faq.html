For Players

Common commands for playing, meetings, and hosting. Some are EHR-specific.

What is Modded Among Us?

Modded Among Us adds new roles, abilities, and ways to win. A lobby might have Crewmates, Impostors, Neutrals, Coven roles, and add-ons.

Some mods also include extra game modes, like free-for-all (FFA).

Every mod handles roles a little differently. A kill does not always mean someone is an Impostor, and voting someone out is not always the right move.

Yes, even Crewmates can sometimes kill.

Do I need to install the mod to join a lobby?

For a host-only mod, you can usually join through the regular Among Us game. Only the host needs to install it.

Other mods, including Town of Us Mira, may require everyone to install files first. On supported devices, you can use Starlight Launcher on Android or follow the mod’s PC installation guide.

What is a modded region?

A modded region is a community-run server you can choose instead of an official Among Us region. Hosts sometimes switch regions when modded lobbies are being kicked or blocked by anti-cheat.

Niko regions are often used for host-only mods such as EHR and Project Lotus. Other modded regions may be needed for mods that require an installation on every player’s device.

Check which region the host is using before joining. The lobby code alone is not enough if your region is different.

How do I join a modded region on mobile?
  1. Open Among Us.
  2. Open mauq.xyz/servers or au.niko233.top.
  3. Pick the region your host is using, such as Niko NA or Niko EU.
  4. Tap the region link and allow it to reopen Among Us.
  5. If it does not appear in the region list, try opening the same link a second time.
  6. Open Create a Game to check your available regions.
  7. Look for the Niko or other custom region you selected.
  8. Select the exact region your host is using.
  9. Return to the lobby screen and enter the host’s code.
How do I join a modded region on PC?

If you installed a mod, it may already include the regions you need. Otherwise, visit au.niko233.top and follow its instructions for customserver.bat.

Error or domain issue?

  • Delete MiniRegionInstall from BepInEx/plugins.
  • Download the custom server BAT file.
  • Launch the file.
  • Press the button it asks for.
  • Relaunch the game.
Which commands should I learn first?

These are the most useful basics for regular players:

  • /m or /cmd m to read your role and add-on details.
  • /r to list active roles, or /r [role] to read one role.
  • /id or /cmd id to see player IDs.
  • /kc or /cmd kc to see how many killers are still alive.
  • /death or /cmd d after death to see death information.
  • /w [ids] [message] for meeting whispers when your role allows it.

In meetings, use the /cmd prefix for guessing, whispering, judging, and other hidden role actions so you do not expose yourself in public chat.

How does guessing work?
  1. Use /cmd id to find the player's ID.
  2. In the meeting, use /cmd bt [ID] [RoleName].

Most mods show player IDs during meetings. If you cannot see them, run /id before guessing.

For example, to guess player 0 as Dictator, type /cmd bt 0 dictator. Double-check the ID and role first; guessing rules depend on the mod and lobby settings.

Some modded clients also provide a guessing interface during meetings, so you may not need to type a command.

Why do I keep getting guessed?

You might be giving away more information than you realize. Players who know the roles can spot clues in how you move, vote, or use abilities.

Chat messages and commands can be clues too. Asking for an ID and immediately trying a meeting ability can make your role easier to figure out.

Read your role with /m and check whether guessing is enabled before using a role-specific ability in public.

Players with similar role colors may be on your team, but always check your role instructions before assuming that.

If someone has an icon beside their name, use /icons to check it. The icon might indicate a relationship, such as a Lawyer or Executioner target.

What if I die?

Do not leave immediately after dying. You can still chat with other ghosts, and some roles have abilities or win conditions that continue after death.

Why does my lobby code say it cannot find the game?

First, check that the lobby is still open and that you copied the code correctly. Then compare your region with the host's. A correct code on the wrong region can still fail.

If you are using a custom region, reopen its link, select the region in Among Us, and try again. Ask the host whether the lobby has already started or closed.

How do I know which region to choose?

Use the region the host lists with their lobby code. NA, EU, and custom-region labels are not interchangeable, even when the code looks valid.

If the host did not say which region they are using, ask before changing game files or reinstalling anything.

A kill does not always mean evil. Crew killing roles like Sheriff, Vigilante, or Crusader can exist.
If your role and name color match another visible player, you are often on the same team. Do not guess or kill teammates unless your instructions say otherwise.
If you froze, black-screened, lost chat, or could not vote, it may be role-related rather than a normal game glitch.
Use /m in game before asking public role questions. It is one of the easiest ways to avoid getting guessed early.

For Hosts

These are the most common questions for hosts who use mods.

How do I update the mod normally?

Download the correct DLL for your mod version and replace the older file in Among Us/BepInEx/plugins. Close the game before changing files.

For example, to replace an EHR test build:

  1. Open BepInEx.
  2. Open plugins.
  3. Delete the old EHR.dll.
  4. Paste the new EHR.dll in.
What are good autohost settings?
  • Initial countdown: about 10 seconds.
  • Delay before entering: about 5 to 10 seconds.
  • Forced start: around 1.5 to 3.
  • Minutes before autostart: around 1.5 to 2.
  • Enable Autostart in the mod settings behind the gear icon.
  • If you plan to be AFK, do not forget to enable Game Master.
How do queue and code tools work in MAUQ?
  • /thread rename to rename a lobby thread.
  • /thread close to archive and lock a lobby post.
  • /code set to set the private code.
  • /queue toggle to turn queue on or off.
  • /queue next to ping the next players and send the code.
  • /queue remove to remove someone without pinging them.
  • /code remove then /code set to change the code.
  • /blacklist toggle (member) to add someone to the blacklist.

More advanced queue settings can be found on mauq.xyz.

What is the current known host ban or kick issue?

If your lobby is repeatedly getting kicked or flagged, these workarounds were suggested for a previously reported issue. They may not fix every server or game version:

  • Play with 9 or fewer users.
  • Stick to mini-games that do not have a kill button.
  • Use Niko regions as mentioned above.
How do I read logs when something breaks?

Use these tools before guessing at the problem:

  • EHR Log Analyzer for EHR logs.
  • MAUQ Log Reader for other mod logs.

Custom Tags

How does everyone have pretty names in Among Us? I want one too.

1

Create the tag

Go to moddedamong.us/tags, fill out the form, and download the generated .txt file. Make sure you enter your friend code.

2

Send it to the host

After downloading the file, send that .txt file to the lobby host.

3

Name it correctly

The host must make sure the filename exactly matches the player friend code, for example friendcode#1234.txt.

4

Place it in the data folder

Move the file into the mod user-data folder. For EHR, for example: EHR-Data > User-data > friendcode#1234.txt.

Custom tags are host- and mod-dependent, so exact setup can differ between mods.
The filename must match the friend code exactly or the tag may not load.
In EHR, hosts can also use /addtag and /deletetag, but that is separate from the file-based method above.

Common Support Topics

These are some of the most commonly asked support questions.

How do I downgrade Among Us for the mod?

On Steam, open Among Us → Properties → Betas and choose public-previous, if that option is still available and matches the version your mod needs.

If you see a black screen after changing versions, the existing guide suggests removing %userprofile%\AppData\LocalLow\Innersloth\Among Us\settings.amogus. Back up the file first, since this resets local settings.

If you keep a separate modded game folder, copy the downgraded game files into that folder too.

I got kicked for domain mismatch. What do I do now?

Use au.niko233.top. Niko233.me now causes immediate kicks because of the domain migration check.

I am on a guest account and cannot join Niko servers.

Guest accounts are not supported. An account is required.

I have terrible connectivity or cross-region lag.

Try using Cloudflare WARP for stability.

I keep getting kicked as soon as I create a lobby.

Check the lobby’s player limit before creating it. For the issue described here, the suggested limit is 15 players or fewer. It can also help to start a local lobby and check the Impostor count.

How do I install a mod on Linux?

Requirements: own Among Us on Steam and have some patience.

  1. Open Steam and go to your library, then select Among Us.
  2. Click the gear icon, choose Manage, then Browse Local Files.
  3. Download the mod you want for Steam.
  4. Unzip the mod files and paste them into the Among Us folder you opened.
  5. Open a terminal and run sudo apt update, sudo apt install pipx, then pipx ensurepath.
  6. Restart the terminal.
  7. Run pipx install protontricks.
  8. Launch Among Us once, wait for it to load, then close it.
  9. Run protontricks 945360 winhttp.
  10. Back in Steam, open Properties for Among Us.
  11. In General, set launch options to WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%.
  12. In Compatibility, force a specific Steam Play tool and choose Proton Experimental.
  13. Relaunch the game.

The first launch after setup can take 2 to 3 minutes.

Common Among Us error messages

These messages can have several causes, but here is what to check first:

  • Sent # pings that remote has not responded to: Your connection may be dropping. Try a more stable network.
  • ReliablePacketWithoutResponse: The game stopped receiving a response. Restarting the game or changing regions may help.
  • Network is unreachable: Your device cannot reach the server. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • You are running an older version of the game: Make sure your game version matches the mod or lobby requirements.
  • Could not find the game you’re looking for: Check the lobby code, region, and whether the game is still open.
  • SteamworksAuthFail or AuthFail: Make sure Steam and Among Us are running under the correct account.
  • Bad Gateway or authentication errors: The problem may be on the server’s side. Retry after a short break.
  • Network Packet Loss or Reliable packet was not ack’d: Try another region or a steadier connection.
What should I send when asking for help?

Say whether you are on PC, Android, or another device; which mod and game version you are using; and whether you are playing or hosting. Include the region and the exact error message.

If the problem happens when the game starts or a lobby opens, explain when it occurs. A screenshot or log is useful, but remove lobby codes, account details, and anything private before sharing.
