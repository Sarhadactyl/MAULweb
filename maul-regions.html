What MAUL Regions offer

Levels & rankings

Earn XP from hosting and finished games. Check hosted time, games played, and player rankings.

Reserved lobby codes

Eligible hosts can use their own custom lobby codes, including when remaking a lobby after disconnecting.

Public lobby listings

Hosts can share their lobbies on the MAUL website. Listings can show the code, region, player count, and installed mods.

Host commands

Use #0kcd to set kill cooldown to zero, or #set to adjust vanilla settings where supported.

Lobby handoff

#handoff lets a host queue a player with compatible mods to take over the lobby when they leave.

Player passports

Collect stamps as you join different hosts' lobbies and keep track of your visits.

Mod support

Works with many modded Among Us setups. Public listings can display multiple installed mods and their links.

Lobby privacy & stability

#private hides a lobby from MAUL's public listing. Hosting cooldowns and disconnect handling help keep lobbies running smoothly.

Some commands are host-only or depend on the mod and game version you're using. Public lobby access may require verification.
