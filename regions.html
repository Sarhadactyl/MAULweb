Mobile Steps

  1. Open Among Us.
  2. Click the region you would like to use.
  3. Change your region in the Among Us region selector to the region you are trying to join.

If nothing opens, make sure Among Us is installed and open first.

Download installer
02

Replace regioninfo.json manually

regioninfo.json is maintained by Niko233.

For a transparent, manual setup:

  1. Download regioninfo.json.
  2. Press Win + R and open %userprofile%\AppData\LocalLow\Innersloth\Among Us.
  3. Replace the existing file, then restart the game.
03

Microsoft Store version

regioninfo.json is maintained by Niko233.

If the script does not work on the Microsoft Store version, follow the save-file route:

  1. Download regioninfo.json.
  2. Open %userprofile%\AppData\Local\Packages\Innersloth.AmongUs_fw5x688tam7rm\SystemAppData\wgs.
  3. Find the folder with long numeric names and open the JSON-like save file inside.
  4. Paste the contents of regioninfo.json, save, and restart.
