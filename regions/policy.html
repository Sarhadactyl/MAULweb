Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information MAUL uses when you connect to our custom Among Us regions or use related features such as lobby posting, the live lobby page, reports, notifications, and rankings.

Information we use

Depending on what you use, MAUL may receive or store:

  • Connection and technical information, including IP addresses and request or connection details.
  • Among Us information such as player names, friend codes, account or player identifiers, game version, region, game code, lobby status, player count, language, and mod information.
  • Discord information used for verification or website access, including your user ID, username, and display name.
  • Information created by MAUL features, including lobby posts, notification opt-ins, reports, hosting statistics, cooldowns, moderation records, and abuse-prevention records.

What we use it for

We use this information to run MAUL services, including to:

  • Operate the custom regions and route players between MAUL region nodes.
  • Verify members and lobby hosts.
  • Create, update, and remove lobby advertisements and live lobby listings.
  • Provide notifications, rankings, and hosting statistics.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce bans and MAUL rules, review reports, and protect MAUL systems.
  • Troubleshoot connection problems, crashes, exploits, and other service issues.

Live lobby website

The live lobby page is limited to verified MAUL members. Account setup is confirmed through Discord and is linked to your Discord account.

Passwords are not stored in plain text. MAUL stores a salted password hash instead. When you log in, the site uses an HTTP-only session cookie so you can stay signed in. Sessions expire and can also be revoked.

What other users can see

Discord lobby posts can show the host, region, game code, language, mods, player count, and lobby status. The live lobby page can show the host name, Discord username, game code, region, mods, player count, and status to logged-in MAUL members.

The MAUL rankings page is public and can show public display names with activity statistics such as XP, hosted time, games finished, and lobby totals.

Lobby posting

When a supported MAUL region offers to post a lobby, or a host requests a post, MAUL receives the lobby details needed to verify the host and create the listing. This can include the game code, region, host name, friend code, linked Discord account, mods, player count, language, game version, and lobby status.

Lobby listings are updated while the lobby is active and can be removed automatically when the lobby closes, starts, expires, or is removed by the host.

Notifications

If you choose a lobby notification option, MAUL stores the Discord user ID, game code, and time needed to manage that notification.

Reports & moderation

Reports can include the reporter and reported player's in-game identifiers, names, friend codes, report reason, and lobby information. Where possible, MAUL may match those identifiers to verified Discord accounts so staff can review the report.

MAUL also keeps records needed for moderation and abuse prevention, such as cooldowns, temporary blocks, whitelists, bans, and related staff actions.

Hosting statistics

MAUL can track hosting activity from lobby posts, including how long a verified host keeps a lobby active. Player counts may be used when calculating hosting totals, and staff can make documented corrections when needed for errors or downtime.

Region relay

MAUL operates more than one region node. When relay is enabled, the nodes exchange the information needed to locate and route a lobby. A player connecting through one MAUL node may have their game traffic relayed to another MAUL node where the lobby is hosted.

How long information is kept

Some records are intentionally short-lived. Live lobby entries expire unless they are refreshed, website sessions expire, and login setup tokens are removed after they expire or are used.

Other records, including accounts, verification data, moderation records, reports, and cumulative statistics, may be kept longer when they are needed to operate MAUL, maintain accurate statistics, or enforce rules. The current report system keeps a limited number of recent reports rather than an unlimited history.

Service providers

MAUL relies on services such as Discord and third-party hosting, networking, DNS, security, certificate, and storage providers. Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

Using MAUL regions and related features is optional. You can log out of the lobby website, remove an active lobby post through supported controls, decline lobby posting, avoid notification features, or stop using the service.

Some records may still be kept when needed for moderation, security, ban enforcement, or the integrity of MAUL statistics.

Security

MAUL uses reasonable measures to protect its services and stored information, but no online service can guarantee perfect security or uninterrupted availability.

Using MAUL services

MAUL regions and related services are provided for custom Among Us gameplay and MAUL community features. Follow MAUL rules, region rules, and reasonable lobby rules set by the host.

Website accounts

Live lobby website access is for verified MAUL members. Do not use another person's account, share access to bypass verification, evade a disabled account, or attempt to access restricted website or API functions.

You are responsible for keeping your password private and signing out of devices you no longer control.

Lobby posts

Lobby posting is for genuine, active lobbies. Do not knowingly submit false information, impersonate another host, spam posting requests, misuse reserved or whitelisted codes, or try to bypass posting cooldowns or restrictions.

Reports

Use reporting tools in good faith. Do not knowingly submit false reports, impersonate another player, manipulate report information, or use reports to harass or spam users or staff.

Rankings & statistics

Activity on MAUL regions can contribute to public rankings and service statistics. Staff may correct hosting totals or related records when needed to fix tracking errors, outages, or other obvious inaccuracies.

Prohibited use

Do not use MAUL services to:

  • Attack, overload, disrupt, exploit, or intentionally interfere with MAUL systems or other users.
  • Access accounts, sessions, admin functions, APIs, systems, or information you are not authorized to use.
  • Evade bans, verification requirements, cooldowns, account restrictions, or other abuse-prevention controls.
  • Use malicious automation, malformed traffic, cheats, exploits, or deceptive requests to damage or misuse the service.
  • Engage in unlawful activity.

Conduct & enforcement

Region Admins and authorized MAUL staff may warn, remove, restrict, disable, or ban users when necessary to deal with cheating, harassment, hate speech, threats, disruptive behavior, ban evasion, abuse of MAUL systems, or other serious rule violations.

Enforcement depends on the situation and is not limited to the examples listed here. Access to regions, website accounts, lobby posting, APIs, or other MAUL features may be restricted when needed to protect the community or the service.
