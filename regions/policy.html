Personal data
Information that identifies someone or can be linked to them. A real name is not required: friend codes, Discord IDs, PUIDs, IP addresses, and persistent account-derived keys can be personal data.
PUID, friend code, and platform account ID
A PUID is a Product User ID used by the game's account/authentication system. A friend code is the game's visible account label, such as example#1234. An external or platform account ID, sometimes called an EAID in region tools, identifies an account on a connected game platform. These are different identifiers for accounts, not passwords.
Data ID and temporary player IDs
A data ID is an internal database record number. A region client ID identifies a connection; an in-game player ID identifies a player inside a match. Temporary IDs can change or be reused, so an ID alone is not reliable proof that two events involve the same account.
Client logs
Records of connection attempts or connections, with the time, account identifiers, client/game version, reported platform/mod details, and connection information available to the region.
Chat logs
Text messages and associated timestamps, lobby details, and sender information received through region chat. This can include commands and messages addressed to a specific player where the region records them. These are not a copy of all your Discord messages or other conversations outside MAUL.
Flag logs
Technical or anti-cheat observations: the rule or check triggered, context, severity, time, and associated account/lobby details. A flag is an indicator for investigation, not proof that someone cheated.
Reports, ban history, and moderation/admin logs
A report is a complaint submitted about an incident. Ban history records blocked connection attempts. Moderation/admin logs record actions such as adding or removing bans, reviewing reports or flags, saving private notes, and generating evidence exports. An audit credential label identifies the access used, not necessarily a separately authenticated staff member.
IP hash and pseudonymization
An IP hash is a derived value used instead of a readable IP address in some stored records. Pseudonymization reduces direct exposure but does not make linked records anonymous. A live server and its network provider still need to process the connection's IP address.

Who can see information

The public rankings and their related API show player names and activity totals. The response can also include a persistent account-derived ranking key and last-activity time. Evidence shared for an incident can contain relevant messages, times, reasons, and identifiers; unrelated people and credentials must be excluded.

The MAUL ban list is public. It can disclose banned accounts' friend codes, PUIDs or derived account identifiers, and a short ban category so regions and compatible moderation tools can recognize listed accounts. These identifiers are personal data, even without a real name. Public exports exclude IP addresses, private notes, report text, and chat evidence. Previously imported bans are not automatically republished in new exports unless their origin is confirmed.

A public list can be copied, indexed, or retained in repository history. MAUL can correct or remove its own published entries, but cannot guarantee removal of every third-party copy. When the law requires it, MAUL will take reasonable steps to notify recipients or other controllers about a correction or erasure request. Contact admin@moddedamong.us to challenge an entry.

IP addresses and region logs

MAUL NA and EU use a private-key IP hash in saved client and flag records, IP-ban fields, and blocked-connection history. The management panel displays that hash rather than the readable address. Authorized lookup tools can hash an address entered by the operator to compare it with saved records. A shared private key allows matching between the two MAUL regions.

The region still processes your live IP address to receive and return game traffic. Linode, Cloudflare, and other providers handling a connection can also process the readable address. Their network and access logs are separate from MAUL's hashed region records. Older console logs, backups, or IP addresses someone types into message text are not automatically changed by hashing a connection field.

A keyed hash reduces exposure but remains pseudonymized personal data; it is not a claim of anonymity. MAUL restricts access to the hash key and applies the same retention rules to hashed identifiers. EDPB: pseudonymisation.

These privacy rules cover both MAUL NA and MAUL EU. The region label does not change your rights under applicable data-protection law.

Short-lived feature records

Ordinary session deletion does not preserve the rest of a lobby's chat because one player was reported or banned. Report an incident while the lobby is active; later reports may no longer have surrounding chat available. Cleanup failures are retried hourly. Files left by an interrupted process follow the 30-day event-date backstop; new-format abandoned session chat files are also cleared on restart.

The normal validity periods are 15 minutes for website setup tokens, seven days for website sessions, and ten minutes for an unrefreshed website lobby listing. A listing stays active while the region refreshes it. Used or expired token records, expired sessions, and stale listings are removed on the next hourly cleanup. Logging out deletes the current session. Pending lobby confirmations expire after five minutes; notification opt-ins end when the notification is sent or the related post is removed, with a 30-day backstop for stale records.

Discord conversations and ticket transcripts

Deleting a report from MAUL's stores does not delete the original conversation from everyone's Discord history. Ticket bots can deliver a transcript to MAUL and to the person who opened the ticket. MAUL's downloaded or archived transcript follows the relevant report or appeal deadline. Copies already delivered to another person are outside MAUL's control.

Sarha removes MAUL's own messages and stored attachments where the platform allows this. Discord does not let a recipient delete another person's messages from a direct-message conversation. Those messages can remain in Discord's history until the sender or Discord removes them; MAUL stops using them as an active case record after the applicable deadline. You can ask MAUL to review accessible copies and can use Discord's controls or contact Discord about platform-held records. Updates, backups, and copies

Maintenance and upgrade backups can contain user data as well as source code. Copies on MAUL's devices, in staff channels, and in evidence exports keep the original deletion deadline. Copying, renaming, or restoring a file does not restart it. A backup that cannot be selectively cleared must be removed by the earliest applicable deadline, and restored data must be checked before it is used again.

Specific legal holds

The operator records the reason, scope, and review date for a hold, reviews it every 30 days, and deletes the held copy within 30 days after the obligation or claim ends unless a specific law sets a different period.

Providers may retain separate records for their own legal or security purposes. Their independent records have their own policies. That does not remove MAUL's responsibility to arrange deletion of data a processor holds on MAUL's instructions.

Email admin@moddedamong.us with what you want reviewed and an account reference that helps locate the records, such as a friend code or Discord ID. Do not send a password, game login token, or government ID with the first message.

MAUL can ask for proportionate proof that the account or records are yours where there is a reasonable doubt. Information about other people can be redacted from an access response; "private staff note" alone is not a blanket reason to exclude personal data about you.

For requests covered by GDPR, MAUL must respond without undue delay and within one month. Complex or numerous requests can justify up to two additional months, with an explanation within the first month. A refusal needs reasons and information about complaint and court remedies. You do not have to use a ban appeal to exercise a privacy right. You can complain to the authority for your habitual residence, place of work, or place of an alleged infringement where applicable. The EDPB authority directory lists EEA regulators; the ICO handles UK data-protection complaints.

Automatic checks and human review

Region checks can automatically reject or disconnect a connection, enforce a listed ban, or create a chat/name autoban when a configured rule matches. They use submitted text, account/connection information, and the relevant rule or ban entry. The consequence can be losing access without a person first reviewing that specific event. A flag can be wrong or lack context.

To appeal a ban, contact admin@moddedamong.us or Sarha through Discord within seven calendar days after receiving the ban notice. Include the ban ID if available, relevant account details, and your explanation. Do not send someone else's private information unless it is needed as evidence.

Ask for Sarha to review the actual rule match and evidence rather than repeat the automatic result. That review route is available even where a game-access decision does not meet GDPR Article 22's threshold for legal or similarly significant effects. The seven-day community appeal window does not shorten a statutory right or privacy-request deadline.

Younger players

Users must meet the game and Discord age requirements that apply to them. Public usernames, activity records, and moderation evidence can concern younger players too. Their privacy interests need particular care; a public display name or a game account does not remove that protection.

A parent or guardian can contact the operator about a child's information. Any authority or account check must be proportionate. If a feature needs parental authorization under applicable law, publishing a policy or assuming that every player is an adult does not supply it.

Security

Management access uses authenticated credentials. The current operator is Sarha. Credentials and raw records must not be posted in public repositories, lobby messages, or public support channels. A redacted evidence export is still checked before it is shared, because free text can contain personal information.

Access restrictions, IP pseudonymization, and log cleanup address different risks; none guarantees that every copy is protected or deleted. There is no guarantee of perfect security. Where GDPR requires notice of a personal-data breach, the operator must assess and make the required regulator and affected-person notifications.
