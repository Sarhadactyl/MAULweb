Privacy Policy

This section explains what information MAUL may receive, use, store, display, and retain when you use the Service.

Information MAUL may collect

Depending on what you use, MAUL may receive or store:

  • Connection and technical information, including IP addresses, request details, connection details, timestamps, and region node information.
  • Among Us information, including player names, friend codes, account or player identifiers, PUIDs, data IDs, game version, region, game code, lobby status, player count, language, and mod information.
  • Discord information used for verification, login, moderation, or website access, including user IDs, usernames, display names, and linked account status.
  • Information created by MAUL features, including lobby posts, notification opt-ins, reports, hosting statistics, rankings, cooldowns, bans, whitelists, and related staff actions.

How MAUL uses information

MAUL uses information to operate and protect the Service, including to:

  • Run MAUL Regions and route players between MAUL region nodes.
  • Verify members and lobby hosts.
  • Create, update, and remove lobby advertisements and live lobby listings.
  • Provide notifications, rankings, hosting statistics, and public service statistics.
  • Review reports, enforce bans and MAUL rules, prevent abuse, and protect MAUL systems.
  • Troubleshoot crashes, connection problems, exploits, and other service issues.

Public and member-visible information

Discord lobby posts can show the host, region, game code, language, mods, player count, and lobby status. The live lobby page can show the host name, Discord username, game code, region, mods, player count, and status to logged-in MAUL members.

The MAUL rankings page is public and can show public display names with activity statistics such as XP, hosted time, games finished, and lobby totals. MAUL does not show admin-only identifiers, including IP addresses, on public website pages or post them in public Discord channels.

NA/EU privacy and region logs

MAUL uses the same privacy controls across both MAUL NA and MAUL EU and works to keep those practices aligned with GDPR principles. MAUL treats IP addresses, friend codes, PUIDs, data IDs, Discord IDs, and similar account or player identifiers as personal data. These identifiers are used to operate the Service, investigate reports, prevent abuse, enforce bans, and troubleshoot connection or exploit issues.

MAUL limits access to management panels, ban tools, raw logs, and lookup tools to the MAUL operator by default. If access is later given to trusted staff, it is limited to people who need it for moderation, security, or technical support. MAUL avoids collecting or displaying more personal information than is reasonably needed for those purposes.

Where raw IP addresses are not needed, MAUL pseudonymizes them with server-side keyed hashes in logs and management views. Active IP-ban records may store the raw IP address when it is reasonably needed to enforce a ban or protect the Service.

Retention

Some records are intentionally short-lived. Live lobby entries expire unless they are refreshed, website sessions expire, and login setup tokens are removed after they expire or are used.

Raw client logs, chat logs, flag logs, and similar admin logs are intended for moderation, abuse prevention, ban appeals, troubleshooting, and service security. By default, MAUL deletes raw client, chat, and flag log files after 30 days. Records needed for an active report, ban appeal, security issue, rule enforcement, or service integrity may be kept longer when reasonably necessary.

Other records, including accounts, verification data, moderation records, reports, and cumulative statistics, may be kept longer when they are needed to operate MAUL, maintain accurate statistics, enforce rules, or protect the Service.
