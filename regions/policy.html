Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains information that may be processed when you connect to or use a custom Among Us region operated by MAUL. Use of the MAUL custom regions is optional.

Information processed

To operate the region, the server may process technical, account, connection, and gameplay information, including:

  • IP addresses and network connection information.
  • Among Us player or account identifiers, including PUID-related information and friend codes where provided by the game.
  • Player names, game versions, platform or client information made available to the server.
  • Lobby information, game codes, player counts, game events, and server events.
  • Moderation, abuse-prevention, security, and diagnostic information.
  • Server logs generated while operating or troubleshooting the service.

How information is used

Information may be processed to:

  • Connect players and operate Among Us lobbies.
  • Maintain the availability, performance, and security of the regions.
  • Route connections between MAUL-operated region nodes.
  • Prevent abuse and enforce applicable MAUL rules.
  • Investigate crashes, connection problems, exploits, or other technical issues.
  • Diagnose and improve the reliability of the service.

Inter-node relay

MAUL operates regional server nodes. When inter-node relay is enabled for a lobby, information needed to locate, register, and route that lobby may be exchanged between MAUL-operated nodes. Players connecting through another MAUL node may have their game traffic relayed to the node hosting the lobby.

Logs & retention

Technical, security, moderation, and diagnostic logs may be retained when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, or maintain the service. Retention may vary depending on the information and why it is needed.

Infrastructure

MAUL may use third-party hosting, networking, DNS, security, certificate, or infrastructure providers to operate the regions. Those providers may process network or service information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

Connecting to a MAUL custom region is optional. If you do not want your information processed as described here, do not connect to or use a MAUL-operated custom region.

Security

MAUL may use reasonable technical and operational measures intended to protect the region and information processed by it. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security or uninterrupted operation.

Terms of Service

By connecting to or using a MAUL-operated custom Among Us region, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the region.

Use of the service

The regions are provided for custom and modded Among Us gameplay. You are responsible for how you use the service and for following applicable MAUL rules and any rules established by a lobby host.

Prohibited use

You may not intentionally use the region to:

  • Attack, disrupt, overload, exploit, or interfere with the servers or other players.
  • Attempt unauthorized access to server systems, administrative functions, or other users' information.
  • Evade bans, technical restrictions, security controls, or abuse-prevention measures.
  • Use automation, malformed traffic, exploits, or other methods intended to damage or destabilize the service.
  • Use the service for unlawful activity.

Behavior & Region Admin discretion

Players are expected to behave appropriately while using MAUL-operated regions. Region Admins may, at their discretion, warn, remove, restrict, or ban players from the regions when their behavior is disruptive, abusive, unsafe, or otherwise inappropriate.

This may include, but is not limited to:

  • Hacking, cheating, exploiting, or intentionally abusing game or server mechanics.
  • Using slurs, hate speech, harassment, threats, or other abusive language.
  • Inappropriate, disruptive, or intentionally offensive behavior.
  • Attempting to interfere with lobbies, players, Region Admins, or region services.
  • Evading a region ban or other restriction.

Region Admins may consider the circumstances and severity of the behavior when taking action. Not every form of inappropriate behavior can be specifically listed in this policy, and enforcement is not limited only to the examples above.

Access & enforcement

MAUL may restrict, suspend, or block access to the regions when reasonably necessary to protect the service, enforce rules, address abuse, or maintain server stability.

Availability

The regions are provided as available. Servers may be restarted, changed, limited, moved, temporarily unavailable, or discontinued. Compatibility may also change when Among Us or supported mods are updated.

Mods & third parties

MAUL custom regions may be used with third-party Among Us mods, launchers, tools, or services. Unless specifically stated otherwise, those third-party projects are independently operated and are governed by their own terms, policies, and support practices.

Service changes

Features, commands, relay behavior, supported versions, networking, and other parts of the region may change as the service is developed or maintained.
